# Changelog

## 2.1 · 2026-11-24

- Added four headwords: trader, digital content, improper means and employ. Reviewed 26 senses across 25 headwords against named authorities. The other 8,620 senses retain their inherited review status.
- Distinguished the England and Wales and U.S. federal tests for summary judgment. Corrected the subjective element previously attributed to dishonesty. Added the U.S. trade-secret test and UK statutory meanings of consumer and goods alongside existing meanings. Preserved the EU scope on the earlier data-subject sense when adding the UK sense.
- Audited Version 2 at commit 18293ef8ebbb6b1126a64fc3c8a2c7891502287d. Its 8,227 individual records had unique stable IDs and unique case-folded headwords, 8,633 senses, and 8,790 cross-references with existing target IDs. In 807 references the display label differed from the target headword. Some are inflections and some lead to a different concept.
- Redirected nine demonstrably misdirected references, including National Insurance under Pay As You Earn and child arrangements order under adoption. Collapsed 26 identical repeated references within records. Version 2.1 has 8,764 references and 793 non-identical label/target pairs counted by occurrence. Semantic review of the inherited links remains open. Cross-reference count is not a count of reviewed relationships.
- Added sense-level sources, jurisdiction and review guidance, and reproducible Markdown rendering. A lookup may use plain text search or the standard-library command in the README. The release adds no independent dictionary, hosted service or database.
